TERMS AND CONDITIONS
  1. This permit is valid only on the dates and time specified above.
  2. Approval of Work Permit at least two (15) days BEFORE actual work schedule and from 09:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Saturday only.
  3. A strict 'No Approved Work Permit, No Work' policy is in effect.
  4. Work permit must be presented to the Guard-on-Duty for access on any area.
  5. A copy of this work permit inserted in a plastic sleeve must be posted on the door of the unit during the whole duration of work.
  6. All workers must use the specified access of workers to Commercial Units.
  7. Workers must present their Company ID (Contractor ID) will be surrendered to the security upon entry to the building. In exchange, a Temporary Workers Access Pass will be worn by the workers and site engineers whenever they are within the building premises.
  8. Construction debris and other garbage such as crates and Styrofoam, which are not accepted by garbage collector for hauling, shall be disposed of by the unit owner/tenant every day after the work hour is finished, and will not be disposed in the garbage bins , expenses and penalties of which shall be for the account of the unit owner/lessee if receptacles of construction are seen inside the bins.
  9. For hot works the Unit Owner / Contractor shall provide fire Extinguisher at the job site and to observed the safety Guidelines all the times and apply separate work permit for hot works.
  10. Loitering in the common areas is prohibited.
  11. All workers are bound by the Rules and Regulations of One Corporate.
  12. Workers wearing sleeveless shirts, shorts, sandals or slippers and the like shall not be allowed to enter the premises.
  13. For any additional Construction rules and regulation's, Please refer to the sent General Constructions Guidelines.
