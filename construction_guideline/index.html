1. General Provisions & Objectives

Governance Standard

The Rules and Regulations relating to repairs, improvements, and other construction (hereafter referred to as the “GENERAL CONSTRUCTION GUIDELINES”), adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Condominium Corporation pursuant to the Master Deed with Declaration of Restrictions, govern and regulate the use and occupancy of individual units and common areas.

  • Ensure efficient and orderly management: Promote the health, safety, and welfare of all unit owners and occupants.
  • Peaceful & Quiet Enjoyment: Protect the right of all unit owners to quiet enjoyment of their respective units and common spaces.
  • Structural Soundness & Aesthetics: Maintain the structural integrity, building safety, and visual appearance of the property.
  • Property Value Enhancement: Preserve and enhance long-term asset value.
  • Technical Standardization: Provide a clear technical baseline for design, material quality, and installation standards.
Authority & Delegation

The Board of Trustees has full authority to enforce these guidelines. Authority is delegated to the Property Management Office (PMO) and the Building Maintenance Office (BMO), with technical plan review conducted by FCLaranang Development Corporation Engineering & Technical Services Division.

2. Synopsis of Key Pre-Requisites

Essential Requirements

Before any fit-out, renovation, or repair work can commence, contractors and unit owners must satisfy four essential prerequisites:

  1. Construction Cash Bond & Insurance: Deposit monetary bond (₱50,000 or 20% of project cost) and submit CGL insurance policy (if available).
  2. Contractor Authorization & Forms: Submit Appointment Letter of Contractor, Authorized Signatories Sheet, Specimen Signatures, Worker ID List, and Gate Pass.
  3. Renovation Application & Drawing Submission: Submit formal renovation application along with 4 sets of A3 signed & sealed drawings.
  4. Fee Payments: Settle Plans Review Fee (₱20,000) and Monthly Monitoring Fee (₱5,000/month) at the PMO Accounting Office.

4. Technical Specifications & Guidelines

Engineering Standards

4.1 Structural & Architectural Restrictions

  • Chipping & Coring: All structural chipping works are subject to BM approval. No welding on structural reinforcement (columns/shear walls). Coring floor slabs or structural members is strictly prohibited without written authorization.
  • Fire Resistance Rating: All interior construction materials must possess at least a 1-Hour Fire Resistance Rating. Fire Retardant certificates must be submitted to PMO.
  • Mezzanine Prohibition: Installation of any mezzanine structure is strictly prohibited for all units (sold or leased), including file storage.
  • Unit Inclusions: Pre-installed tiles, toilet fixtures, glass partitions, and switches cannot be removed/altered without prior written consent and an undertaking to restore upon lease termination.

4.2 Drywalls & Ceiling Standards

Component Allowed Materials & Rules Prohibited Materials
Drywall Partitions Non-combustible studs & boards. Must terminate at finished ceiling level. Wall insulation must be non-combustible. Plywood partitioning with wood nailers (combustible). Concrete nails on walls.
Ceilings Non-combustible, sound absorbent, lightweight, non-toxic, non-asbestos gypsum on metal frame. Wood nailers, wood framing, asbestos materials. Ceiling supports attached to sprinkler pipes or light fixtures.
Floor Tiles Must conform to building design standards and be level with existing elevator lobbies. Unapproved slab chipping, boring, or deep chasing without structural clearance.

4.3 Electrical Requirements & Specs

  • Power Supply: Provided via pull box for 1-phase 230V or 3-phase 400V (refer to single line diagram). Branch circuits must be balanced across 3 phases.
  • Wires & Conduits: Minimum wire size is 3.5 mm² THHN (Phelps Dodge or equivalent rated 600V). EMT conduit for exposed works; PVC Series 1000 for embedded works. Flexible metallic conduit for ceiling light fixtures.
  • Circuit Protection: Bolt-on type circuit breakers manufactured by ABB, GE, or approved equivalent, fully coordinated with base building protection.
  • Illumination Levels: Standard office desk work requires 500 - 1,000 lux illumination in compliance with OSH Standards.
  • Water Heater Limit: Maximum allowable design load for water heaters is 30 Amperes per unit.
  • Sign & Seal: All electrical plans and load calculations must be signed and sealed by a Professional Electrical Engineer (PEE).

4.4 Mechanical & Fire Protection Systems

Fire Protection Engineering Baseline

Sprinklers are standard 1/2" orifice chrome-plated recessed heads rated at 155°F (68°C). Maximum coverage is 130 sq. ft. per sprinkler head (and at least 1 head per enclosed room). Minimum clearance between lighting/A-C diffusers and sprinkler heads is 1.0 foot (12 inches).

  • Air Conditioning: Split or Multi-split units allowed only on designated refrigerant pipe provisions to maintain building exterior uniformity.
  • Fire Alarm System: Addressable smoke detectors (100 sqm coverage area per head) wired in EMT conduit fastened directly to slab. Wiring & devices must be 100% compatible with base building FADS.
  • Sprinkler Alterations: Relocations must be performed only by PMO-accredited certified fire protection contractors. Sprinkler pipes must never be used to support wood cabinets or light fixtures.

4.5 Sanitary & Plumbing Specifications

  • Water Lines (Cold & Hot): Polypropylene Pipe PPR PN-20 (Brands: Unitec, Belden, or white-colored equivalent).
  • Waste & Vent Pipes: Polyvinyl Chloride PVC Series 1000 (Brands: Atlanta or equivalent).
  • Comfort Room Location: Comfort room / toilet locations are fixed and cannot be relocated from their base building provision.

5.2 Worker Dress Code & Badging

All contractor personnel must surrender valid company ID at Security for an ACCESS PASS (pinned to upper left shirt). Personnel must wear company uniform, safety shoes/boots, and PPE.

Ejection Warning

Workers wearing slippers, sleeveless shirts, muscle shirts, or shorts will be refused entry or immediately ejected from the building.

5.3 Strict Working Hour Restrictions & Noisy Works

  • Noisy Works Window: Noisy works (chipping, heavy drilling) must be scheduled AFTER 5:00 PM to 6:00 AM only.
  • Noisy Works Exception: If noisy work is critical to schedule completion, owner/contractor must submit an Engineering Control Plan (noise barriers, quieter equipment) to PMO/BM for approval prior to work.
  • Stay-ins Prohibited: Construction sites must NOT be used as sleeping quarters or stay-ins. Overtime must be pre-arranged with the Building Manager.

5.4 Fire Safety, Hot Works & PPE

  • Portable Extinguishers: Contractor must provide 1 unit 10 lbs HCFC 236 FA portable fire extinguisher (green tank) for every 11.4 feet (4 meters) radius of work space.
  • Hot Works & Welding: Requires a daily Hot Works Permit from BMO, Lockout Tagout (LOTO) compliance, and a dedicated FIREWATCH person present during welding.
  • Prohibited Activities: Smoking, cooking, drinking alcohol, gambling, horseplay, drug use, weapons, and pets are strictly prohibited inside the building.
  • Debris Management: Construction debris must be hauled out of the building nightly during the 6:00 PM - 12:00 MN delivery window. Debris left overnight results in bond penalty deductions.

6. Post-Construction & Construction Bond Refund

Project Sign-Off & Closeout

6.1 Final Inspection & Deficiencies Sign-Off

Upon completion of fit-out work, the Unit Owner/Tenant must formally request a Final Inspection in writing from the Building Manager. Any deficiencies noted during inspection must be rectified immediately by the contractor.

6.2 As-Built Plans Submission Requirement

30-Day As-Built Deadline

To qualify for a Construction Bond refund, the unit owner/tenant/contractor must submit Four (4) complete A3 sets of signed and sealed As-Built Plans within 30 calendar days of project completion and acceptance.

6.3 Construction Bond Refund Workflow

  • Refund Timeline: Construction cash bond is refunded two (2) months after project completion and final acceptance.
  • Deduction Criteria: Full bond is refunded provided no violations were committed, no property damage occurred in common areas or adjacent units, and all penalty fees are settled. Net refund is released after applying all valid deductions.

7.1 Notice of Violation (NOV) Protocol

  1. Inspection & Discovery: Building Maintenance conducts daily site audits and documents deviations.
  2. Formal NOV Communication: Written Notice of Violation issued immediately to Unit Owner and Contractor.
  3. Immediate Work Stoppage: PMO and BMO reserve the right to HALT ALL CONSTRUCTION IMMEDIATELY until non-compliance is resolved.
  4. Financial Deduction: Penalty fees are deducted directly from the ₱50,000 cash bond.
