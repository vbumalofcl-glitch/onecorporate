Dynamic Report Generator — Instruction Manual

User guide on Report Types, Target Date periods, included/excluded log rules, and export options
🎯 How the Dynamic Report Engine Filters Data

When you select a Report Type and set the Target Date / Period Starting, the generator dynamically calculates the calendar date boundaries to aggregate matching checklist items, inspection logs, running hours, fuel metrics, and photos.

1. Daily Maintenance Report
Exact 1-Day Scope
Target Date Selection: Pick the exact inspection day (e.g. 2026-07-20).
  • What it Contains: Routine timeline checklists (06:00 AM – 09:00 PM), before & after photo evidence logged on that date, individual operational sub-checklists, tenant tickets resolved that day, building manager oversight checks for that specific date, and matching daily maintenance procedure logs.
  • What is Excluded: Multi-day historical records and systems that have zero inspection entries on this specific date.
2. Weekly Summary Report
7 Consecutive Calendar Days
Target Date Selection: Pick the Starting Date of the 7-day reporting cycle (e.g. 2026-07-20 covers 2026-07-20 to 2026-07-26).
  • What it Contains: 7-day cumulative task completion metrics, technician leaderboard, weekly compliance percentage, tenant complaints resolved during the week, full before/after photo gallery, and the Summary of Daily Activities (day-by-day flight check logs, running hours, and fuel consumption registers).
  • What is Excluded: Tasks and audit records outside the 7-day calendar window.
3. Monthly Compliance Report
Full Calendar Month
Target Date Selection: Pick any date in the target month (e.g. picking 2026-07-15 automatically spans 2026-07-01 to 2026-07-31).
  • What it Contains: Comprehensive monthly building compliance overview, monthly/quarterly preventive maintenance execution logs, cumulative running hours and fuel/energy metrics, spare parts replacement records, and tenant work order resolution summaries.
  • What is Excluded: Day-to-day timeline checklists from other months.
4. Annual Building Condition Report
Full Calendar Year (365 Days)
Target Date Selection: Pick any date in the target year (e.g. 2026-07-20 evaluates 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
  • What it Contains: Full 12-month facility condition scorecard, major system overhauls, load bank testing certifications, annual elevator & fire protection statutory compliance certificates, yearly parts consumption ledger, and executive engineering sign-offs.
  • What is Excluded: Hourly routine checklist details not relevant to annual executive audits.
⚠️ Core Logic: "If No Log History, No Report"

To guarantee clean, professional compliance documents, only systems with verified inspection records or reading logs during the selected period are rendered in the report preview. Uninspected systems are automatically omitted to prevent empty dummy tables from appearing in official audits.

Log Category / Source Inclusion Condition When It Is Omitted
Routine Checklist Tasks
Daily shift scheduled checks		 Date matches target period OR task has attached photo evidence Created/completed outside selected period dates with no photos
Before & After Photo Evidence
Inspection & defect photos with captions		 Always included whenever photos/captions exist for tasks in period When photo toggle is unchecked or task has no uploaded photos
Tenant Complaints / Desk Orders
Tenant tickets and remediation actions		 Filed or resolved within the period date window Tickets filed outside selected date range or when section is unchecked
System Procedure Compliance Logs
Genset, Fire Pump, STP, Chiller, etc.		 System is checked AND has at least 1 logged audit on target date System unchecked OR system has 0 recorded inspection logs
Run Hours & Fuel/Energy Metrics
Engine meter, diesel level, burn rate		 Equipment reading logged for the matching date/cycle Toggle disabled or no equipment meter log exists
Parts & Consumables Swaps
Filters, belts, lubricants, wear parts		 Parts replacement logged during period Toggle disabled or zero parts replaced in period
🎛️ Report Section Toggles Guide

You can customize the exact contents of your generated report by enabling or disabling section checkboxes before clicking Generate Report Preview:

📷 Before & After Pictures with Captions:
Embeds side-by-side photographic comparisons of defect findings and corrective repairs with engineer remarks.
📋 Maintenance Requests & Tenant Complaints Log:
Outputs all unit tickets, tenant names, reported defects, and documented maintenance resolutions.
🔍 Operational Sub-Checklist Audit Log:
Includes granular point-by-point checklist items for all inspected systems with individual Pass/Defect indicators.
👨‍💼 Building Manager Oversight Audit Log:
Attaches the time-slotted building manager supervisory compliance checklist and completion percentages.
⚙️ Maintenance Procedures System Selector:
Selectively include any of the 9 core MEPF systems: Genset, Fire Pump, STP, Domestic Water, Submersible Pumps, Housekeeping, Chiller/AHU, Electrical Substation, and Elevator Safety.
⏱️ Equipment Running Hours & Fuel / Energy Metrics:
Appends generator/pump start meter readings, end meter readings, period operating hours, fuel levels, and burn rates.
✍️ Digital Signatures & Print Layouts
1. Attaching Signatures

Official reports require two sign-offs: Reported By (Lead Engineer) and Approved By (Building Manager).

  • Click ✍️ Digital Signatures in the preview header.
  • Choose either ✏️ Draw Signature (canvas pad), 📁 Upload Image (transparent scan), ⌨️ Type Signature (script styling), or select from 💾 Saved Presets.
  • Click Attach Signature to Report. Both signatures will render at the bottom of the official document.
2. Continuous PDF vs. Multi-Page Print Layout
📜 Continuous PDF (Auto-Fit Height)

Creates a single unbroken long-sheet PDF without artificial page cuts. Ideal for digital audits, tablet viewing, and messaging/email attachments.

📄 Standard Multi-Page

Formats the document into standard paginated A4 / Letter sheets with headers and page breaks for physical binder printing.
