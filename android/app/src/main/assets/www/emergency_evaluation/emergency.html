The Golden 3-Minute Rule: Why Defined Roles Matter

Statistical emergency data demonstrates that the first three (3) minutes of an incident are the most critical. Having an active, role-designated Building Emergency Response Team (BERT) prevents incident escalation, eliminates confusion, and minimizes life safety risks.

3 Min Initial Golden Action Window

Universal 6-Step Emergency Response Sequence

Every commercial building emergency follows this structured 6-step lifecycle regardless of hazard type:

1

Alert

Discover emergency, sound alarms, pull pull-station, dial 911 immediately without waiting.

→
2

Assess

Building Emergency Coordinator (BEC) & Controllers evaluate hazard scope and structural risk.

→
3

Notify

Broadcast PA voice announcement, mass text/email, and activate internal emergency phone tree.

→
4

Respond

Execute Evacuation / Shelter-in-Place / Area of Refuge and deploy First Aid Triage.

→
5

Account

Floor Wardens sweep zones and report headcount roll calls to Assembly Area Marshal.

→
6

Recover

Conduct physical ATC-20 audit, issue official All-Clear, and file After-Action Report (AAR).
