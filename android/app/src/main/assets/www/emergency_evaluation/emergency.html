The Golden 3-Minute Rule: Why Defined Roles Matter

Statistical emergency data demonstrates that the first three (3) minutes of an incident are the most critical. Having an active, role-designated Building Emergency Response Team (BERT) prevents incident escalation, eliminates confusion, and minimizes life safety risks.

Every commercial building emergency follows this structured 6-step lifecycle regardless of hazard type:

1

Alert

Discover emergency, sound alarms, pull pull-station, dial 911 immediately without waiting.

→
2

Assess

4
